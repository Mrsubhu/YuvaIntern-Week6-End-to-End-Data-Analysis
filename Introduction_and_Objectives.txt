1. INTRODUCTION

Online retail businesses generate transaction data that can help explain
sales performance, product demand, and customer purchasing behavior.
Analyzing this data can help businesses identify patterns, monitor
performance, and make informed operational decisions.

This project uses the Online Retail dataset and follows an end-to-end
data analytics workflow, from initial data exploration to predictive
modeling. The work consolidates the analyses performed during Weeks 1–5
of the internship.

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The main objectives are:

1. Explore the dataset and understand its structure and data quality.
2. Identify missing values, duplicate records, and invalid transactions.
3. Prepare a cleaned dataset for analysis.
4. Visualize sales trends and product performance.
5. Apply statistical methods to examine relationships and differences.
6. Build and evaluate a machine learning model for sales-related
   prediction.
7. Convert verified analytical findings into practical business
   recommendations.

3. PROJECT SCOPE

The project covers transaction-level retail data, including invoice
dates, products, quantities, unit prices, and customer identifiers
where available.

The analysis is limited to the fields and time period available in
the dataset. Findings will be interpreted in the context of the
dataset and the methods used.
